# Privacy Policy for Smiley Arena

**Last updated:** November 21, 2026

## Introduction

Smiley Arena is an online player-vs-player arena game. This privacy policy describes how the app and its servers handle data. It applies to the Google Play version of the game.

## Data Collection and Storage

**Guest play.** You can play against bots without signing in. Guest matches are simulated locally on your device: no account is created, and no match results or personal profile are stored on our servers. The app requests a short-lived guest token from our server, so the server sees standard connection data (such as your IP address). Your local match counters, language and sound settings are saved on your device only.

**Sign-in with Google Play Games.** The Google Play version signs you in through Google Play Games. We receive your Play Games player ID, display name and profile picture URL from Google, and use them to create your account and recognize you on return visits. Your display name is used as your in-game nickname and appears in leaderboards. We do not receive your Google password or email address through this sign-in method.

**Account and gameplay data.** For signed-in players we store on our servers: your account ID, login, nickname, provider account ID, profile picture URL, in-game avatar and hat, match history and statistics (wins, losses, damage dealt and received, rating), daily activity, in-game currency and owned items, and session records (a hashed refresh token, app/device user agent, IP address and expiry). This data is needed to run matches, keep you signed in, maintain leaderboards and protect the game from abuse.

**On your device.** The app saves a refresh token in native app storage so you stay signed in, plus small local settings: guest match counters, preferred language and sound settings. Signing out removes the saved token; uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

## Third-Party Services

- **Google Play Games (Google)** is used for sign-in. Google processes your Google account data under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- The Google Play version of Smiley Arena does **not** include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and does not collect an advertising ID.

## Permissions

The app requests the following Android permissions:

- **INTERNET** — required for sign-in, matchmaking, online matches and loading game content
- **VIBRATE** — optional haptic feedback for the fire button

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or files.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Account data is kept while your account is active. Local settings and guest statistics remain on your device until you sign out, clear the app data, or uninstall the app. To request deletion of your account and the data associated with it, contact us at the address below.

## Children's Privacy

Smiley Arena is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.
